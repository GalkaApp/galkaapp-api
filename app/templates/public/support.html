Getting started

  • You do not need an account. Install the app and start typing; everything except sync works offline on one device.
  • To sync, create an account here, then in the app open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Sign in and enter the server address, your email and your password.
  • Quick Add reads the title as you type: tomorrow 3pm sets the date, @Work the project, !high the priority and #urgent a tag. English and Russian both work.

Common questions

My devices are not syncing

Check that both are signed in to the same account, and that the server address in Settings ▸ Account matches on both — a device that was set up against a local server keeps that address until you change it. Changes are pushed a couple of seconds after you stop typing; pull to refresh the list to force a sync.

Two devices edited the same task

The most recent edit wins, and the other devices take it. Nothing is silently merged field by field, so if a task looks out of date on one device, it was edited later somewhere else.

I deleted something by accident

Deleted tasks go to the Trash and stay there until you empty it. Restore is per item, or restore the lot. Once the Trash is emptied the tasks are gone on every device.

Reminders are not arriving

Reminders are scheduled by the system on the device, so they need notification permission — check the system settings for Galka. A reminder fires at the lead time you set in Settings, ahead of the due time.

I forgot my password

Write to {{ contact_email }} from the address the account uses and we will reset it. Your tasks stay on your devices while you wait.

I want my data, or I want the account gone

Ask at {{ contact_email }} from the account's address. An export comes back in a machine-readable format; a deletion removes the account and its content within 30 days. See the privacy policy.

Can I run my own server?

Yes — the server address in Settings ▸ Account is editable, and the sync API is documented. Data on a server you run is yours alone.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, iOS and iPadOS 17 or later. The app is in English and Russian, and the language can be switched inside the app without changing the system language.
