What it does

Plan the day

Today knows about your evening

Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Someday and a Logbook. Anything overdue is called out at the top, and one tap moves all of it forward.

Capture

A task in one line

Type the date, the project, the priority and the tags straight into the title. On the Mac a global hotkey opens the composer over whatever you were doing.

Organise

Projects that hold their shape

Sections, tags, priorities, Markdown notes, checklists and duration estimates. Repeating tasks from daily to a custom rule.

Mac

A board when a list isn't enough

Any project switches to a kanban board — To Do, In Progress, Done — with drag and drop, plus a menu bar mode and launch at login.

Review

See where the time went

The Logbook keeps every completion. Stats show the day, the week, the month, your current streak and your best run.

Everywhere

Widgets, reminders, themes

Home Screen and desktop widgets with today's list and a quick add button. Reminders fire ahead of a due time. Light and dark, English and Russian.

Sync, if you want it

No account required

Galka works entirely offline on a single device — nothing is sent anywhere until you sign in. Create an account and your tasks, projects and history sync between your own Mac, iPhone and iPad, offline edits included.

Your tasks are yours: they are never sold, never shared, and never used for advertising. Delete the account and everything in it goes with it. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Or keep the whole thing at home: the sync server is open source and ships as a Docker image, so you can run it yourself and point the app at your own address.

Moving in from another app?

On the Mac, Galka imports projects, to-dos, notes, checklists, tags, dates and repeat rules from your existing task app in a few clicks — Settings ▸ Data ▸ Import.
